# FarmiQ — Agricultural Advisory Disclaimer

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** November 2026

---

## Statutory Agricultural Disclaimer

FarmiQ provides crop management advisories, pest and disease identification, chemical treatment guidance, and weather-based farming recommendations for **informational and decision-support purposes only**.

---

## 1. ICAR Compliance Notice

Crop growth stage timelines, fertilizer schedules, and farming best practices referenced in FarmiQ are based on recommendations from the **Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)** and State Agricultural Universities. These are general guidelines and may not account for:
- Local soil micro-variations
- Regional climate anomalies
- Specific crop variety requirements

**Always consult your nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or Block Agriculture Officer for field-specific guidance.**

---

## 2. CIBRC Chemical Safety Notice

Chemical pesticide and herbicide recommendations in FarmiQ are advisory only. All chemical applications must:
- **Strictly follow the dosage and mixing instructions on the product label** approved by the **Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC)**
- Observe the **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI / waiting period)** before harvesting treated crops
- Require appropriate **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)** — gloves, mask, protective clothing
- Never be performed in high winds, rain, or near water bodies
- Store chemical containers safely, out of reach of children, in a locked area

---

## 3. Weather Forecast Advisory

Weather forecasts in FarmiQ are sourced from Open-Meteo (satellite-based numerical weather prediction models) and India Meteorological Department (IMD) public data. Forecasts are updated hourly but:
- **Localized micro-climate variations** can differ from displayed data
- **Forecasts beyond 48 hours** carry increasing uncertainty
- Always visually inspect field and sky conditions before major operations (sowing, spraying, harvesting)

---

## 4. AI-Based Crop Damage Analysis

The Crop Check (leaf disease scan) feature uses image analysis to suggest probable crop disease identification. This analysis:
- **Is not a certified diagnostic** and should be treated as a preliminary screening tool
- May not identify all diseases correctly in all lighting and photo conditions
- **Should be verified** with a local plant pathologist or agriculture officer before large-scale treatment
- Natural and chemical remedies suggested are general recommendations — verify with ICAR-published pest management guides

- Crop losses or reduced yields resulting from following app-generated advisories

---

## 6. Emergency Agricultural Support

In case of a severe crop disease outbreak, exotic pest invasion, or agricultural emergency:
- Contact your **State Agriculture Department Helpline**
- National toll-free: **1551** (Kisan Call Centre — Government of India)
- Contact your nearest **Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)**
