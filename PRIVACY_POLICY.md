# FarmiQ — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 21, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Governed By:** Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India)

---

## 1. Introduction

FarmiQ ("the App", "we", "us") is a digital farming assistant application designed exclusively for Indian farmers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and respect the data you provide when using the FarmiQ app.

We are committed to your rights as a **Data Principal** under the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023**.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- Full name, mobile phone number, district/region, preferred language.
- These are collected at registration for the sole purpose of personalizing your farming experience.

### 2.2 Farm & Crop Data
- Farm name, plot sizes (in acres), crop types, varieties, sowing dates, harvest predictions, growth stage logs, and task completion records.
- **This data belongs entirely to you.** FarmiQ does NOT sell, transfer, or share this data with any agribusiness company, trader, advertiser, or third party.

### 2.3 Location Data
- Used **only** to fetch hyper-local weather forecasts and to calculate the 100 km marketplace radius.
- Never stored permanently. Collected only with your explicit permission.

### 2.4 Photos & Images
- Crop damage photos uploaded for leaf disease analysis.
- Stored securely in your account scope. Never shared externally.

### 2.5 Session & Technical Data
- User-agent string (browser/device type) stored with each session for security.
- No advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics SDKs are used.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data

| Purpose | Legal Basis (DPDP Act 2023) |
|---|---|
| Personalizing farm advisories and task schedules | Legitimate interest / User consent |
| Delivering local weather alerts | User consent (location permission) |
| Connecting you with nearby buyers in the Marketplace | User consent |
| Storing crop monitoring logs and financial records | User consent |
| Authenticating your identity securely | Contractual necessity |

---

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- Passwords are **never stored in plain text**. We use **bcrypt** (industry-standard cryptographic hashing) with salt.
- Session tokens are **256-bit cryptographically random** values, hashed with SHA-256, and stored in **HTTP-only secure cookies** — inaccessible to browser JavaScript.
- All database queries use **Row-Level Privacy isolation** — your data is accessible only to your account.
- Database connections use encrypted channels (TLS/SSL in production).

---

## 5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We **do not** sell, rent, or share your personal data with:
- Corporations, traders, or procurement companies
- Advertising networks or data brokers
- Government agencies (except when legally required by a valid court order)

The only partial disclosure: If you list produce in the **Marketplace** and voluntarily check "Show my phone number to buyers," your phone number will be visible to buyers in your 100 km area.

---

## 6. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023 — Sections 11–14)

As a Data Principal, you have the right to:

| Right | How to Exercise |
|---|---|
| **Access** your personal data | Settings → View Profile |
| **Correct** inaccurate data | Settings → Edit Profile |
| **Erase** your account and all farm records | Settings → Delete Account (or contact us) |
| **Withdraw consent** at any time | Settings → Logout / Delete Account |
| **Nominate** a representative for your data | Contact: privacy@farmiq.app |
| **Grievance Redressal** | Contact our Grievance Officer (details below) |

---

## 7. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Account & crop data | Until account deletion |
| Session tokens | 1 year (auto-expire) |
| Crop damage photos | Until manually deleted or account deletion |
| Daily log activities | Until account deletion |

## 8. Children's Privacy

FarmiQ is intended for adult farmers (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has registered, contact us immediately at privacy@farmiq.app.
